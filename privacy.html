Who we are

AI Skills Zone is a Udyam-registered business in India, owned and run by Chandirakumar M, selling self-paced online courses at aiskillszone.com. Chandirakumar M is the person responsible for your data and the one who answers questions about it: write to help@aiskillszone.com. This policy is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your consent

We collect data only when you give it to us for a stated purpose: your email when you ask for a login code or press Notify me, your name and mobile number when you buy. The buy panel says what each field is for before you type into it, and continuing is your consent to that use. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete your data (see below); the course access that depends on the account ends with it.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and login codes), hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Cloudflare (website, course pages, email forwarding for help@).
  • Razorpay (payments). Razorpay collects your payment details directly under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend (sending our emails).
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), for the login codes and purchase confirmations we send to your WhatsApp number.

Each of them handles your data only to provide its service to us. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Account and course access: for as long as your account exists. Access is lifetime, so this is until you ask us to close it.
  • Purchase records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is closed.
  • Notify-me sign-ups: until the course launches and you have been told, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • WhatsApp delivery events: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Buy-window steps: 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Server logs: on the providers' standard short retention.

Your rights, and how to delete your data

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email help@aiskillszone.com from the address on your account and say which you want. We act within seven days and confirm by reply.

Deletion removes your account, your login history, your mobile number, your Notify-me entries and your message delivery events. The purchase record is kept in the minimum form the tax rules require (amount, date, order id) and is no longer linked to a usable account. Deleting your account also ends your access to any course you bought, since the course is tied to the account; we will say so before we go ahead.

Reading progress lives only in your browser; clear the site's data in your browser to remove it.

Login codes and messages

We send one-time login codes by WhatsApp to your mobile number, or by email when you choose email on the log-in page. We send purchase confirmations the same way. We do not send marketing messages on WhatsApp. If you start a purchase and do not finish it, or a payment fails, we may email you once to help. We email news about new courses and offers only to people who ticked the offers box when buying; reply to any such email to stop, and the box is unticked by default. The only other message you may receive is a single notice when a course you pressed Notify me for launches.

Security

There are no passwords to leak: sign-in is by one-time code. Data is stored with access restricted to the service that needs it, payment secrets are kept out of the website entirely, and every payment is verified on our server against Razorpay's signature before a course is unlocked. If we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you by email.

Children

The courses are for working professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has signed up, email us and we will remove the account.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that affects how your existing data is used will be announced by email before it takes effect.

Index
    You needTwo or more years of manual or functional testing. Comfortable with test cases, bug reports, Postman.
    You do not needAny code, any terminal, any Git. Module 2 teaches Python from the first line.
    PaceAbout seven hours a week for 23 weeks. Already write Python? Skip Modules 2 and 3: about 19 weeks.

    What the learner leaves with

    • A GitHub portfolio with six labs and one capstone, each with a README a hiring manager can skim.
    • An eval suite for a chatbot that runs on every commit and fails the build when quality drops.
    • A red-team report with reproducible prompt-injection findings.
    • A test plan template for AI features they can reuse on day one of the new job.
    • A resume rewritten from "manual tester" to "AI quality engineer", and fifty interview answers.

    How the hours were sized

    Lecture hours are watching and following along. Lab hours are hands-on with a definition of done. Project hours are open-ended work marked against a rubric. First-time coders usually spend about a third longer on the labs in Modules 2 to 4, and the weekly pace allows for it.
